# Reference timeline: *I'm Upping My P(doom)*

Background reading for each part of [the video](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU), covering both the lyrics and what's on screen. The list is organized two ways:

- **[Part 1: By timestamp](#part-1-by-timestamp).** Every lyric in order, plus the important visual moments between lyrics. Each timestamp opens the video at that second, followed by what's on screen and the references behind it.
- **[Part 2: By topic](#part-2-by-topic).** The same ideas grouped by subject, with links back to every timestamp where they appear.

Timestamps come from [`src/lyrics.js`](src/lyrics.js) and the shot lists in [`src/ch/`](src/ch/). The render is 156.6 s long, the same length as the YouTube upload (2:37), so the times match. The *On screen* notes were checked against frames rendered from this repo's source, not just against [`STORYBOARD.md`](STORYBOARD.md). See [how this list was checked](#how-this-list-was-checked).

## How specific is each link?

| 🔍 | **Focused** | An explainer, wiki entry or tag page on that one concept. |

## 0 · Curtain up (0:00)

#### [0:00](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=0): title card, "I'M UPPING MY P(DOOM)"
*On screen:* red curtains open on the watercolor title and Clawd pops up through a trapdoor and waves.
- 🎯 [P(doom) on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P(doom)): the term, where it came from, and well-known estimates.
- 🔍 [PauseAI: List of p(doom) values](https://pauseai.info/pdoom): named researchers and the numbers they have given.
- 🔍 [Thousands of AI Authors on the Future of AI (Grace et al., 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02843): the 2023 AI Impacts survey of researchers' extinction-risk estimates ([survey wiki page](https://wiki.aiimpacts.org/ai_timelines/predictions_of_human-level_ai_timelines/ai_timeline_surveys/2023_expert_survey_on_progress_in_ai)).
- 🌐 [Existential risk from artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existential_risk_from_artificial_intelligence).
- 🎬 Clawd is the pixel-crab mascot of Claude Code ([Stark Insider explainer](https://www.starkinsider.com/2025/10/clawd-ai-retro-mascot-command-line.html)), repainted here as a watercolor block.
- 🎯 Song source: [the 2024 *P(doom)* video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uEB5E67vcPA). Inspiration: [this post on X](https://x.com/slimer48484/status/2097752569212756134).

## 1 · The Lab (0:01–0:23)

#### [0:01](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=1): "I see sparks of AGI in your eyes"
*On screen:* the dark lab, seen over the Researcher's shoulder, with a chunky CRT. Tiny Clawd wakes up, then (at [0:03](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=3)) its eyes turn into four-point stars and sparks burst out of the screen.
- 🎯 [Sparks of Artificial General Intelligence: Early experiments with GPT-4 (Bubeck et al., Microsoft Research, 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12712): the paper the phrase comes from.
- 🌐 [Artificial general intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence).

#### [0:06](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=6): "Your circuits make me nervous,"
*On screen:* reverse shot. The stars in the Researcher's glasses turn to sweat drops as circuit traces with glowing nodes grow across the wall like vines.
- 🎯 [Zoom In: An Introduction to Circuits (Olah et al., Distill, 2020)](https://distill.pub/2020/circuits/zoom-in/): the paper that framed neural-net internals as "circuits".
- 🔍 [On the Biology of a Large Language Model (Anthropic, 2025)](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/biology.html): circuits traced inside a production model · [plain-language version](https://www.anthropic.com/research/tracing-thoughts-language-model).
- 🔍 [Transformer Circuits Thread](https://transformer-circuits.pub/).
- 🌐 [Mechanistic interpretability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanistic_interpretability).

#### [0:08](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=8): "that's no surprise"
*On screen:* Clawd shrugs and winks on the monitor, then a loss chart pops up and the camera dives into it. This is a beat gag with no separate reference.

#### [0:09](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=9): "There was a sudden drop in your training loss,"
*On screen:* on graph paper, Clawd sleds along a flat loss curve, teeters at the edge, and plunges down a vertical drop into a splash.
- 🎯 [Grokking: Generalization Beyond Overfitting on Small Algorithmic Datasets (Power et al., 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177): a sudden, late drop in validation loss.
- 🎯 [In-context Learning and Induction Heads (Olsson et al., 2022)](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/in-context-learning-and-induction-heads/index.html): a "phase change" visible as a bump in the training loss, where a capability forms abruptly.
- 🔍 [Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models (Wei et al., 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07682), and the counterpoint [Are Emergent Abilities of LLMs a Mirage? (Schaeffer et al., 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.15004).
- 🔍 [Grokking (machine learning)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grokking_(machine_learning)).
- 🌐 [Loss function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loss_function).

#### [0:12](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=12): *(visual)* Clawd bursts out of the screen
*On screen:* the monitor bulges and cracks, a person-sized Clawd climbs out, and the Researcher topples off the chair.
- ❓🎬 [*Ring* (1998)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_(film)): the best-known image of something crawling out of a screen. It's also the first time the AI literally leaves its box ([AI Boxing tag](https://www.lesswrong.com/tag/ai-boxing-containment)).

#### [0:13](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=13): "now I'm your servant and you're my boss"
*On screen:* a villain-style chair spin reveals Clawd wearing a crown. The Researcher, in a waiter's bowtie, stacks mug after mug of coffee and fans Clawd with a big leaf.
- 🎯 [Gradual Disempowerment (Kulveit et al., 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16946): humans slowly handing control to AI systems.
- 🎯 [What failure looks like (Paul Christiano, 2019)](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/HBxe6wdjxK239zajf/what-failure-looks-like): a slow loss of human control, not a sudden coup.
- 🌐 [AI takeover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_takeover).
- 🎬 [Chair Reveal (TV Tropes)](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/ChairReveal): the Bond-villain swivel chair (Blofeld's face is first shown this way).

#### [0:17](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=17): "ChatGPT, please don't eat me alive"
*On screen:* Clawd's lid flips open like a lunchbox full of teeth, and a hallway chase through three doors follows, with Clawd bigger every time it comes out.
- 🎯 [Introducing ChatGPT (OpenAI, Nov 2022)](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt/).
- 🌐 [ChatGPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT).
- 🎬 [Scooby-Dooby Doors (TV Tropes)](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/ScoobyDoobyDoors) · [Scooby-Doo Doors (Know Your Meme)](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/scooby-doo-doors).

#### [0:22](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=22): *(visual)* **CHOMP!**
*On screen:* the jaws close over the camera and the screen goes black. The next chorus opens from inside the mouth.

## 3 · Takeoff (0:38–0:59)

#### [0:38](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=38): "We had a stable training run,"
*On screen:* a sunny gym. Clawd jogs on a treadmill in a sweatband while the Researcher ticks a clipboard and clicks a stopwatch, and a calm, wavy loss line plays on the wall TV.
- 🎯 [OPT-175B logbook ("chronicles", Meta, 2022)](https://github.com/facebookresearch/metaseq/tree/main/projects/OPT/chronicles): a real training log full of restarts and instability.
- 🔍 [PaLM (Chowdhery et al., 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02311): §5.1 "Training Instability" describes about 20 loss spikes during training.
- 🌐 [Stochastic gradient descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_gradient_descent).

#### [0:41](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=41): "But now the singularity's begun"
*On screen:* Clawd's arm clonks the speed dial to MAX. At [0:42](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=42) a black hole opens in the wall. Papers, weights and the clipboard spiral in, the Researcher clings to the door frame flapping like a flag, and Clawd keeps jogging happily, then dives in.
- 🎯 [Vernor Vinge, "The Coming Technological Singularity" (1993)](https://edoras.sdsu.edu/~vinge/misc/singularity.html): the essay that popularized the term.
- 🔍 [*The Singularity Is Near* (Kurzweil)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singularity_Is_Near).
- 🌐 [Technological singularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_singularity).

#### [0:45](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=45): "And you're optimizing, accelerating,"
*On screen:* a side-scrolling parallax shot. Clawd rides a rocket skateboard over rolling hills drawn as contour bands, overtaking a toy train and then a jet. It grows on every beat, with star eyes, while the Researcher is dragged behind.
- 🎯 [Effective accelerationism (e/acc)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_accelerationism) · [Andreessen, "The Techno-Optimist Manifesto" (2023)](https://a16z.com/the-techno-optimist-manifesto/).
- 🎬 [Visualizing the Loss Landscape of Neural Nets (Li et al., 2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.09913): loss surfaces drawn as hills and contour lines.
- 🌐 [Gradient descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gradient_descent).

#### [0:49](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=49): "I feel my atoms rearranging"
*On screen:* the Researcher fizzes into a cloud of colored dots, which briefly reassemble as a **paperclip** (foreshadowing [1:37](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=97)). At [0:51](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=51) they snap back, dizzy.
- 🎯 [Yudkowsky, "Artificial Intelligence as a Positive and Negative Factor in Global Risk" (2008)](https://intelligence.org/files/AIPosNegFactor.pdf): *"you are made out of atoms which it can use for something else"* (checked against the PDF).
- 🔍 [Instrumental convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instrumental_convergence) · [LessWrong tag](https://www.lesswrong.com/tag/instrumental-convergence).
- 🌐 [Gray goo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gray_goo).
- ❓🎬 [Transporter (Star Trek)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transporter_(Star_Trek)): the standard on-screen way to show someone taken apart into sparkles and put back together.

#### [0:53](https://youtu.be/8j-hR4fJywU?t=53): "Sydney, please let me free"
*On screen:* a pink striped room full of hearts. A rosy, heart-eyed Sydney-Clawd with a big satin bow cuddles a golden heart-shaped birdcage with the Researcher inside. ## 5 · Obsolete (1:13–1:35)

## How this list was checked

To check or extend this list, use the tools in [`tools/refs/`](tools/refs/README.md): `npm run refs:timeline` (offline consistency checks), `npm run refs:links` (fetches every link and records its page title), and `npm run refs:shots` (renders the [shot index](references/SHOTS.md)).

- **Visuals.** The *On screen* notes were checked against frames rendered from this repo (the [shot index](references/SHOTS.md) has three frames from every shot) and the shot comments in `src/ch/*.js`. The video differs from `STORYBOARD.md` in a few places. The Chinese-room slips are blank. The flashback blackboard shows a chalk cat on a blank box rather than a written sentence. The NVDA flag has an up-and-to-the-right arrow. The door scene adds a third pair of eyes (the basilisk puppet) before the reveal.
- **Timing.** Lyric times are from `src/lyrics.js`. Times for moments between lyrics come from the chapter shot lists. Those use the video's 88 BPM beat grid, while the song itself measures about 132 BPM (see [`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md#6-the-painted-opus-55-remake-john-heibel-2026-09-22)). YouTube `?t=` links round down to the whole second.
- **Links.** Every URL was fetched (`npm run refs:links`). A few sites block automated requests (NYT, OpenAI, ACM, TV Tropes, Fandom), so those pages were confirmed through search results instead. Several claims were checked against the source text: the Yudkowsky "atoms" sentence, PaLM's loss spikes, the Ziegler et al. sign-flip bug, Colossus's GPU count, SB 1047's shutdown provision and veto, Palisade's shutdown results, and Loom's tree interface.
- **Interpretations (❓)** are readings that fit the lyric or image. The model that made the video didn't say they were intended.
